Billing questions

How do I pay?

By card, monthly, through our secure payment provider. We never see or store your card number.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Open Billing in your dashboard → Payment method & invoices → Cancel. Your server stays until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens if a payment fails?

We email you and your server keeps running for 3 days while you update your card. After that it's paused until the payment goes through. Billing questions: billing@treehost.online.

What CPU do your servers run on?

Tree Host launches on an OVH RISE-S dedicated server with an AMD Ryzen 7 9700X (8 cores, 16 threads, up to 5.5 GHz), DDR5 ECC memory and mirrored NVMe SSDs. Minecraft's main game loop runs on one thread, so fast single-core speed matters more than core count. We never sell more memory than the machine has.

Can I have more than one server?

Yes, as many as you like. Every monthly plan runs any number of servers, and each one gets the plan's full memory, CPU and disk.

What is the Lifetime plan?

You pay once and keep it forever: 4 servers of any type and version, 2 proxies (Velocity, BungeeCord or Waterfall) and priority support. No monthly bills.

Can I move to a bigger plan?

Yes. Buy the bigger plan, contact support and we'll move your world over, then cancel the old plan.

Do you offer refunds?

See our Terms of Service. If something went wrong, contact us and we'll sort it out.
