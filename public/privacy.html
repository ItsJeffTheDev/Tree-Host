Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what Tree Host collects when you use Tree Host and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, email address, and optionally your Minecraft name. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication: we never see or store your password.
  • Payment details: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record of your plans and whether they are paid.
  • Your servers: the worlds, files, plugins and settings you put on Tree Host, and the server console logs (which include the names of players who join).
  • Technical data: IP addresses in our logs and for rate limiting, and an activity log of actions on your servers (such as starts, stops and backups).

How we use it

  • to run your servers and your account, and to take payments;
  • to send you service emails (password resets, failed payments, important changes);
  • to keep the Service secure and stop abuse;
  • to help you when you contact support (our staff can see your servers to help with problems).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the providers we need to run the Service: Google Firebase (sign-in and our account database), Stripe (payments), our email provider (service emails), and the company that hosts our servers. They may only use it to provide their service to us. We may also share data when the law requires it.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in, and Firebase keeps your sign-in in your browser's storage. We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while you have an account. Servers of ended plans are deleted 14 days after the plan ends. Payment records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can see and change your account details in the dashboard. You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account by contacting us. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California) you may have more rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to a data protection authority.

Children

If you are under the age required in your country to agree to this yourself, a parent or guardian must agree for you.

Contact

Questions or requests: our support address.
