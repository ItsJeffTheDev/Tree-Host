Frequently asked questions

How do I join my server?

Copy the Join address at the top of your server's page (like play.treehost.online:25600). In Minecraft: Multiplayer → Add Server → paste it as the Server Address.

How do I make myself an operator (OP)?

Add your Minecraft name in Account, then click "OP me" on your server's Console tab.

"You are not white-listed on this server"

Click "Whitelist me" on the Console tab (after adding your Minecraft name in Account), or turn the whitelist off in Settings → Game settings.

How do I install plugins?

Plugins tab → Browse plugins → search → Install, then restart the server. You can also drag and drop your own .jar files.

My server stopped with "ran out of memory"

It needed more memory than your plan has. Remove heavy plugins or mods, lower the view distance in Settings, or move to a bigger plan.

Which server types are supported?

Paper, Purpur, Folia, Spigot, CraftBukkit, Fabric, Vanilla, Velocity, Waterfall and BungeeCord, on any version. Forge and NeoForge are not supported yet.

I forgot my password

Click "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page and follow the email. No email? Check your spam folder.

How do I cancel?

Billing → Payment method & invoices → Cancel. Your server stays until the end of the period you paid for.
