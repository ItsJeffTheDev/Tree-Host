# Billing

How plans, payments and canceling work. Open **💳 Billing** at the bottom left of the dashboard.

---

## Plans

- One plan = **one server**. The plan decides the server's memory, CPU and disk.
- Plans are billed **monthly**, starting the day you buy them, by card through Stripe. We never see your card number.
- Want another server? **Billing → + Buy a plan**. Each plan renews and cancels on its own.

---

## Have a code?

Got a code from Tree Host (for example as a creator, partner or giveaway winner)? Open **Billing**, type it under
**Have a code?** and press **Redeem**. The plan is yours right away, for free; create your server from Billing.

Some codes give a plan for a set time (a week, a month, a year...): Billing shows **Free until** and the date.
Others are for life and show **Free (lifetime)**: they never run out. When it ends, the server stops and
we email you: buy a plan within 14 days to keep the server and its worlds.

## Change your card, see invoices, cancel

**Billing → Payment method & invoices** opens Stripe's secure page, where you can:

- update your card,
- download invoices and receipts,
- **cancel a plan**.

When you cancel, the server keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for. After that it is stopped,
and **14 days later the server and its backups are deleted**.

> **Tip:** before canceling, download anything you want to keep (your world folder, configs) from the **Files** tab.

---

## If a payment fails

1. We email you and the plan shows **Payment failed** in Billing.
2. Your server **keeps running for 3 days**. Update your card in **Payment method & invoices** and Stripe retries.
3. After 3 days without payment the server is stopped. It starts again as soon as the payment goes through.

---

## Moving to a bigger plan

1. Buy the bigger plan in **Billing**.
2. Email [billing@treehost.online](mailto:billing@treehost.online): we'll move your world and plugins to the new plan's server.
3. Cancel the old plan in **Payment method & invoices**.
