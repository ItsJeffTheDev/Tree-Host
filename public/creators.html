    Where to find it

    Your creator code was in the email from the Tree Host team when you were accepted. It's the same code that's in your referral link — everything after ?ref=.

    Your earnings

    They're tied to your account, not just the code: after looking up your code, log in with the account you signed up through your link and your balance, paid-out total and recent activity appear right here. Payouts are sent by our team on request, by PayPal, IBAN or gift card.

    Apply to the program

    Applications are reviewed by the Tree Host team, usually within a couple of days.

    What we look for

    Minecraft content of any size — an SMP series, a modpack spotlight, a tutorial, a server list. We care that your audience actually plays, not how big the channel is. No follower minimums to start.

    The fine print

    Commission is a cut of the first payment from each referred player. It lands in your Tree Host balance the moment their server goes live, and you can request a payout at any time — we pay out by PayPal, IBAN or gift card. If a first payment is refunded, the commission is clawed back automatically.
